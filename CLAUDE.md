# StreamOrchard brand

The logo, banner and wording for the **Stream-Orchard** GitHub organization (github.com/Stream-Orchard). `README.md`
has the files, colors and wording; `concepts/` holds the logos that weren't picked.

## Rules

- **PNGs are rendered from the SVGs.** After changing `logo.svg` or `banner.svg`, render the PNG again at the same
  size (1024×1024, banner 1280×640) and check it at 20px as well as full size.
